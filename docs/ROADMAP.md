# Roadmap

- **Numerical validation:** Cross-compiler tests, sanitizers, convergence charts,
  improved LSM basis and out-of-sample continuation evaluation.
- **Market infrastructure:** Dates, day counts, calendars, multi-curve
  bootstrapping, market instrument conventions and quote ingestion.
- **Process engine:** Term-dependent correlation, Heston and jumps,
  quasi-Monte Carlo and optional parallel execution.
- **Alternative pricing engines:** Nonuniform PDE meshes, Rannacher smoothing,
  trees, Fourier methods and stochastic-rate callable instruments.
- **Products:** Bermudan swaptions, callable bonds, multi-asset barriers,
  pathwise coupons and schedule-based contracts.
- **Calibration and risk:** Butterfly-arbitrage-checked SVI/SSVI, Heston and
  Hull–White swaption calibration, volatility surface sensitivities,
  cross-gamma, AAD and stress frameworks.
